# Dolgeville Railroad ROW Wall

[[Little Falls North Side Cliffs|Guide]] · [[Little Falls North Side - Routes|Routes]] · [[Little Falls North Side - Access|Access]]

**Approx. GPS:** [43.03894, -74.84072](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ldk92upnkqenMerT8?g_st=ic)

## Topo & Photos

![[Little Falls North Side - Dolgeville Railroad ROW Wall - Photo.png]]

The ROW wall(s) as you approach from the east

I believe this area was named the He Man Slabs in the 1990 guide, but I have renamed it.  There are small walls on each side of the railroad ROW.  The wall on the north side (right side in the photo) has the better climbing.  As of Fall 2008 there are three routes along the north side wall, with hangers at the top for setting a top rope.  If you climb to the top of the south wall and walk south a few feet there is a drop off where more climbs might be possible.

To get here, park in the pullout up on highway 5, then walk back west about 200 ft to where the path goes south.  Follow the path for about 50 ft until it drops down onto the old ROW.  Turn right (west) and walk a couple hundred feet until you come to the cliffs shown above.  This wall is on the large private land parcel.
