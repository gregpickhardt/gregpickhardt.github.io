### 141 – 145.  Reserved

### 146.  Skin Wax Crack  5.10b/c PG*

Here at last is that rare climb where the crux is actually easier for small people! Climb blocks to the obvious off-hand crack in a pod-like left handed corner. Once you have struggled to the halfway ledge, step right to a thin crack in the narrow buttress above.  Climb up (5.8+ R) using a variety of techniques.  Protection in the upper part can be awkward to arrange.

### 147.  Substitute Teaching  5.10b/c G

The daunting off-finger crack just right of Skin Wax Crack is, unfortunately, ruined by easy access to simple footing on the right.  For those with a strong sense of moral purpose. the crack is a worthwhile exercise and definitely easier for big people!  The cruddy mantle onto the mid-way ledge is actually harder than the crack -- no matter where you put your foot.  Finish on Skin Wax Crack or rap off the tree.

### 148.  Crud Corner  5.6 PG

Not that bad. really. but very short.

### 149.  Face Off In Albania  5.11 Sport

Four bolts to anchors.  Climb the narrow face using the arête and some thin ledges on the face.  The Buzz Junky crack is off limits.

### 150.  Buzz Junky  5 .7 G**

A good crack exercise for the beginning leader.  Climb the obvious finger/hand crack, veering left at the Y-junction.  Continue up to the shared anchor with Face Off in Albania

Randy Big Dog on Buzz Junky; Gray Watkins belays.  Bolt line for Face Off in Albania on the left

### 151.  The Reagan Years  5.10a PG**

It keeps moving senselessly to the right!  Climb the first 20' of Buzz Junky. but traverse right at the horizontal hand crack.  Snake up a thin crack, then step right, up again and veer right into the main crack system.  Climb an awkward corner to rappel anchors shared with Harder than Your Husband. Protection can be a bit hard to arrange in the middle.  A variation is

V1.  Citizen Cohn  5.11b PG  Another Berzins lead.  Take the direct start to the main crack. Thin fingers, laybacking and stemming will help.

### 152.  Harder Than Your Husband  5.9 G**

A nice climb up a naughty corner. The first thirty feet of this perfect corner have been climbed by laybacking. jamming, stemming. “unching” and every other known technique. Step left at the top to the rappel anchors shared with The Reagan Years.

### 153.  T2  5 .7 Sport

Two bolts to anchors.  Good practice for beginners.

### 154.  T5  5 .7 Sport

Two bolts to anchors.  More good practice for beginners.  Start at the crack about eight feet right of T2.  Angle left as you climb and end up at the T2 anchor.

### 155.  Trojan Elite 5.7 G**

Solid protection. Very short but very nice.  Find the perfect finger crack in a beautiful face fifty feet right of the Harder than Your Husband corner.  Should be able to use the Thanador anchor to lower off.

### 156.  Thanador  5.9 Sport**

Four bolts to anchor.  A surprisingly fine climb on the brown face just right of Trojan Elite.  Climb the center of the brown face 5' right of Trojan Elite on obvious holds.  A difficult move will get you to a good horizontal crack. Climb up and right. making a high step onto a shallow. diagonalling ledge which brings you to the top.  Stay away from the Trojan Elite crack.

### 157.  CSX  5.7 Sport

Four bolts to anchor.  Start a few feet right of Thanador and follow the bolt line, moving left at the top to the shared anchor.
