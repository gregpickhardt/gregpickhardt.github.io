# Tier of Fear

[[Little Falls North Side Cliffs|Guide]] · [[Little Falls North Side - Routes|Routes]] · [[Little Falls North Side - Access|Access]]

**Approx. GPS:** [43.03597, -74.83179](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MkTiprFPkEbSExRq9?g_st=ic)

## Topo & Photos

![[Little Falls North Side - Tier of Fear - Topo.png]]

Walk west 100' past the Dihedrals and look for an obvious tree terrace about mid-way up the cliff and left of some broken buttresses and chimneys. Bushwhack to a right-facing corner at the east end of the terrace and climb up the corner onto the tier.  Two webbing handholds have been placed to make the climb a little safer.

### 175.  Racing Dog 5.10a  Sport**

This climb is actually below the tier.  Three bolts to anchors.  Locate a clean beige-colored slab directly below the Tier of Fear, starting up a short, right-facing corner. The opening mantle is harder if you are short. Right-trending ledges will lead you to the buttress which is turned at the top.  An easier finish (5.7) goes left to a thin crack, or climb the buttress (5.11 +) without turning the comer.

### 176.  Ms. Adventure  510b G*

A short. but excellent climb which was put up on the lead.  Very awkward to top-rope because of the bushes at the tope Climb the slightly diagonal very thin crack to a challenging mantle at the lip of a bulge.

### 177.  Beginner's Luck  5.3 G

The left of a pair of slanting. stepped cracks. The easiest way off but too bushy for easy top-roping.

### 178.  Easy Affair  5.3 G

The right: of the two rounded corners.

### 179.  Small Happy  5.8 G*

Another short beauty. Climb straight up a series of Intersecting cracks. then pass a gap in holds by staying slightly right. A good jam crack above leads directly to a bushy and often muddy mantle.  A variation is

V1.  By-Pass  5.7 G  Work your way up on Easy Affair to a diagonalling dike. Traverse right to the jam crack and up to finish.

### 180.  Thought Police  5.9 R*

You're going to have to relax your mind on this one or you'll be blowing dirt out of your ears.  Climbs the face to the right of Small Happy by using two horizontal cracks. a layback (the only pro) and a gripping stretch right.

### 181.  Zig-Zag Crack  5.5 PG

Follow the Z-shaped crack to a squeeze chimney.

### 182.  Fuzzy Tights  5.6 PG

Lose 25 lbs. and try this one again.  Go directly up the chimney in the center of the terrace.  Since getting stuck is probably more of a problem than falling out "full figure" climbers are advised to move out to the front of the Chimney at the midway point and face climb to the top.  A variation is

V1.  Girl on a String  5.10b G*  Almost a bouldering problem.  Jam the off-finger, slightly overhanging crack just 5 ft right of Fuzzy Tights to a good terrace.  Step outside and left to the black arête where a couple of moves will bring you to the top.

### 183.  Head Cement  5.7 G

The very obvious right-facing flake and corner system at the center of the terrace.  Start at the bottom of the corner making a surprisingly awkward move out under a roof to reach a small ledge with a tree stump.  Choose the right technique and pump the crack above until an easy mantle puts you over the top.  A variation is

V1.  Glue Font Spin  5.8 G*  Two steps right of the corner a thin crack leads to a small ledge. Make a couple tricky moves off the ground to a funny mantle on a triangular hold. Step left to the tree stump ledge.

### 184.  Trick Bag  5.11b/5.12b Sport**

** bolts to an anchor.  This route is the subject of frequent sieges and few successes.  The "height-impaired" will find its difficulties a full grade harder than those over 5'11”.  Quite sustained for such a short route.  Start by following a razor thin crack past a bolt and a peg to reach a good horizontal edge. Step left to a second bolt. then power up the bulging wall above. passing another fixed peg and making a dynamic move for the top. Excellent.

### 185.  The Arrow  5.8 G*

A superb off-hand crack. Climb straight up to a squeeze chimney and the top. Easier or harder depending upon the size of your hand.

### 186.  Risqué Shift  5.9+ G**

A short classic.  Climb The Arrow until you can step right under a black roof to a small ledge  A thin vertical crack in a shallow. left-facing corner, leads to better holds.  Jams and other maneuvers will take you to the anchor at the top.

### 187 -188.  Reserved
