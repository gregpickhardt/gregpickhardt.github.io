I don’t think it is really a cesspool; rainwater just runs in from the west end of the cliff and sits here.  Still, it is not very pretty.  The pond drains through a pipe under the railroad, but the pipe is set too high so there is always water in the pond.  All the cesspool pond cliff and the pond itself are on the large private parcel.

The easiest way get to the top of the cesspool cliff is to park up above it (north of it) in the small pullout on the side of highway 5.  This pullout will hold maybe 5 vehicles.  Walk back west 200 ft along the highway where there is a trail going south.  This trail wanders first right then left and drops onto the old railroad ROW.  At this point, if you turn back left (east) Recycle Rock is about 200 ft away (immediately below the cars).  For now though, turn right and walk along the ROW a few hundred feet.  Just before you come to the place where there are small cliffs on both sides of the ROW (there are climbs here too), turn left and you can walk to the top of the Texas Peninsula.  There is nothing harder than some 4th class scrambling.  There are some nice trees near the edge to rappel from.  For the other cesspool cliffs you just have to go back east.

## Texas Peninsula

### 38.  Angle Dust  5.8 G

A short, off-width crack that lends itself to good technique (many climbs do). It is ten feet left of the giant boulder on the escape ledge.

### 39.  Mr. Clark meets the Burrito Master  5.10c TR*

A Randall-Skibinski original.  Short but very sweet.  Climb the scoops, seams and small roofs on the rounded corner/face just right of Angle Dust.

### 40.  Earth to Doris  5.10b G**

On the left side of the "Washbasin" a pair of parallel cracks lead to a tree stump.  Climb the left crack to the slot, than swing over the blocks above to reach the top.

### 41.  Crucial Groove  5.9+ G**

Deceptive and fine.  Climb the wonderful off-hand crack (5 8) on the right side of the Washbasin, taking one step left at the 'T' and working your way up the diagonalling crack above.

### 42.  Good Friday  5.8+ PG**

Easier for tall people.  Climb the hand crack (5.8)-and step right at the 'T'. making a long mantle on the edge to reach better holds. Owing to the traverse; there is some potential for a pendulum fall on this route.  A variation is

Var1  Mud-Sucking Tootsie Roll Eater  5.11b TR  Climb up between the crack and the rounded edge (Mona Lisa Love Muscle), then step left and climb up the right side of the steep face directly above the 'T'.  Highly contrived but thoroughly enjoyable.

### 43.  Mona Lisa Love Muscle  5.10d TR*

Several hard mantles on the rounded edge will connect you with either of the two routes above.

## Center Cliffs

### 44.  Unnamed  5.5 TR

The steep-sided open book. Somewhat ruined by a huge dead tree low on the route and a boulder pile at the top.

### 45.  Chin Music  5.8 G**

Well worth the trouble of leading.  Locate the climb from above by hunting for a 2" crack in the middle of a blueberry patch on a flat ledge just east of the boulder pile.  A hanging belay at the bottom can be easily established from a birch growing out of a crack 8' above water level.  After a few moves around some blocks the route follows a perfect hand crack through two small overhangs and a corner.

### 46.  Unnamed (No information)

Fight your way over a roof in a small corner ten feet above the water.  Work up and left past a bulge in a triangular corner to a small ledge in another shallow corner. Thin cracks lead straight up to slots and the top. Continuous and hard.

### 47.  Rutting Hog Dance  5.11a TR*

Rap down Indiana Nose Pick to water level.  Climb a short hand crack in the left wall, then step out and around the corner at a horizontal crack to confront a short. black face cut by horizontal seams.  Stay somewhat right, making difficult moves into a shallow  right-facing grove with a mantle to the top.  The best belay is from a group of small birches.

### 48.  Indiana Nose Pick  5.7 G*

The large left-facing corner with a tree on the half-way ledge. Begin at water level. Surprisingly awkward near the top.

### 49.  Lady J  5.8 G*

This off-width chimney is short but confining.  A couple large Friends will protect it but it's easier just to top-rope. The route is best located from above by peeping down behind two small birches at the edge of the Carpophobe terrace.

### 50.  Carpophobe  5.10b/5.11b TR**

An excellent route. the difficulty of which depends upon the exit you take. Rap down to the pile of blocks at the bottom of Lady J. Traverse right to reach a diagonalling finger-crack cutting a 2' roof. A couple hard moves on the crack and a delicate mantle puts you beneath the big roof.  Variations are

Var1  Poof Roof  5 11b**  Climb left-center over the big roof.

Var2  Chinese Logic  5.10d*  Right of the roof is a bucket.  Mantle on it to the top.

Var3  Gasp Clasp  5 I0b*  From the bucket stretch right to the arête. A daring clasp or two will get you up.

### 51.  James Brown’s Paradox Philosophy  5.7 G**

Rap to water level and climb a superb lay-back crack in the right-facing corner.  A variation is

Var1  Goof Proof Roof  5.10c TR  Instead of stepping out left at the top. step right and try the roof.

### 52.  Geomancer 5.11+ TR

Exhausting and thin.  Rap to water level and climb the center of the orange gently overhanging face to the right of James Brown’s Paradox Philosophy. After the bulge finish by swinging Goof Proof Roof.

### 53.  Celibate Catfish  5.7+ G

The right-facing corner grove at the end of the Carpophobe terrace. Sometimes a bit dirty.

### 54.  My Daddys at the Pit  5.11b/c R**

Ming Lin found a test-piece for tall people on this squatty, pinched and overhanging route.  Two rusted pegs mark the belay point just above water level.  After the first bolt traverse and climb the left side of the buttress.  A variation is

Var1  5.11 d PG  Take a crooked line just right of the arête powering past two bolts to an exhausting finish.

## Florida Peninsula

### 55.  Project X  5 8+ PG

Start in an open book at water level left of the peninsula.  Stem the corner to a blocky ceiling which is turned on the right by means of a slot.  Move up and then left through another slot to reach the tree ledge above.

### 56. First Blood  5.9+ PG**

Put up on the lead in February, this fine route has a variety of face and crack techniques. Climb an obvious open book with a slot, laybacking onto a narrow ledge.  Shuffle left a couple steps then face climb straight up to a slanting terrace beneath a large roof. Step back right and work up a short crack above.

### 57.  Stump the Droids  5.11a PG**

Use the same start as First Blood, but continue up the initial corner to a terrace on the right.  Work your way into the V-slot on the left side of the big roof, then up to the "Mental Block."  A couple moves on the arête will get you out the top.

### 58.  Fair Weather Friend  5.9-5.10b TR

Saved from greatness by access to too many good holds on the right. Oh well.  Start in the left of two chimneys and escape onto the face (left) when flakes lead you that way.  Avoid buckets on the right and make some hard reaches to gain the small platform just right of a big roof.  End by either climbing straight up or by stepping way left to an exposed mantle.

### 59.  Easy Out  5.1 G

The squeeze chimney to the right of Fair Weather Friend.

### 60.  Bromadosous  5.10a G*

Contrary to its name, you will need good feet to make this climb work.  Climb the face and cracks just right of Easy Out, making a weird move into the "Butt' and then jamming the finger crack above to finish.

### 61.  Butt Woman from Hell  5.10c TR**

Savage and strange.  Start on the face just right of an outside left-facing corner (see sketch) above a flat terrace.  Make a few bouldering-type moves to reach a horizontal crack and better holds.  Follow intersecting cracks to a wide roof which is passed on the right.  Establish yourself in the 'butt" (that's right) by means of peculiar technique, then reach for thin folds to surmount the second roof.  A mantle to bushes completes this short but memorable route.

### 62.  (No information)

The crack/off-width on the left side of the broken face to the right of the Butt Woman from Hell terrace.
