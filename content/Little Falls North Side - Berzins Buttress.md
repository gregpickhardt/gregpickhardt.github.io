The next cliff east is Berzins Buttress.  The ownership of the Wasted Youth Wall, Berzins Buttress and the series of cliffs to the east (Amtrak Arete, Smith Rock, Tier of Fear and the Dihedrals) is not completely, although they are probably on the private parcel.  The most accessible source of information is the Herkimer County GIS map.  If you wish to see the map, go to http://herkimercounty.sdgnys.com/search.aspx.  The ID for the private parcel of land is 115.70-1-1 which you can enter into the search box.  You can look at either the tax map or the GIS map; the latter is the most appropriate because it shows both the property boundaries and the aerial photography.

If you play around for a while you can see that at the western end, the old gravel factory is on the private parcel; the boundary between the private land and the CSX ROW appears to be 15 – 20 ft feet south (closer to the tracks) of the edge of the factory.  As you go east from the factory the boundary runs more or less parallel to the tracks.  You can’t really pinpoint the face of the cliffs in the aerial photography, but the property line appears to be close to the face.  If you go very far away from the face of the cliff you will be on the CSX ROW; if you climb a route and walk north a ways you will be on the private parcel.

While it seems likely that routes such as Doktor Ratt and Haggis have been climbed before, it is ever more certain that no one has ever looked at the rock between these two lines in quite the same way as Martin Berzins.  The superb rock, sustained difficulties and challenging protection of all three played perfectly to Berzins' strengths.  Always strident. almost Pentecostal in his zeal for "a job done right,” Berzins opened not only three excellent routes on this 50' face but also more than a few minds.

### 133.  Doktor Ratt  5-9*

Jam and layback a strange crack in the obvious right-facing orange colored corner above some large blocks.  When the main groove turns left. continue straight up by connecting finger cracks and mantling into bushes.  A worthwhile route.

### 134.  Easy Goer  5 12d***

The hardest of Berzins' three superb routes on this face.  Easy Goer was unseconded at the time and has not as yet seen a subsequent ascent. Locate a choppy, all too obvious finger crack in the orange wall just right of Doktor Ratt.  Reach into the crack and struggle upward until a reach right for a tweeker will allow a desperate snatch to a slightly better hold.  From the ramp continue with interest to gain the easier crack above.

### 135.  America Without Tears  5.11c PG**

Intriguing climbing.  Odd moves on the right side of an obvious black pillar lead to a swing and mantle and an excellent stance from which to contemplate the sobering moves above. Place your protection in a thin crack to the right but then move left and climb a small corner to a horizontal seam which is traversed back right. One more hard move here will bring you to a dilemma: the placement of a solid nut where you've just placed your hand!  A perfect finger-pocket leads to easier moves above.

### 136.  Sunday Silence  5.12a PG**

Who but Berzins would have found a route here?  Begin in the shallow open book just a few left of the Haggis corner and just right of America Without Tears.  Protect in the cracks to your left and right before you mantle onto an awkward block beneath a bulge. Another tricky move leads to a relatively good stance to place a small wire (shaky) in a shallow diagonal crack.  This piece protects the very thin crux move above.  Stay with the obvious crack to better pro and the top.

### 137.  Haggis  5.5 PG

Less pleasant. Climb straight up the obvious right-facing corner that divides the main face in half.

### 138.  Billy Tipton  5.8+**

Looks can be deceiving.  Different than it appears and a much better route than it looks to be from the ground.  Climb the face and crack just right of Haggis to a slot and sloping ledge, then directly up the excellent crack above.

(Billy Tipton was a family men and a ,jazz musician. When he died in 1988 his autopsy revealed that he was actually a woman).  Variations are

V1.  Autopsy Surprise  5 10a*  Take on the finger crack at the bottom directly.

V2.  Tenor’s Gasp  5.10b*  Struggle into the right-facing corner and follow it over a small triangular roof to reach the sloping ledge.

### 139.  Motor Box  5.9+ R

Not quite great.  Climb a short right-facing corner under the big roof.  Step out and make a tricky more over the roof (5.8. poor pro) to establish yourself on the sloping ledge above.  Follow a thin crack until you can step right to a bushy ledge.  Two moves short of greatness.

### 140.  Noq Tube Heat  5.11? TR

Start by bouldering the front of the large block below the big roof. Step up to the black arête above. Same thin moves on the left side of the arête will get you to easier ground.
