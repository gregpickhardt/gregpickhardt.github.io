# Smith Rock

**Approx. GPS:** [43.03605, -74.83205](https://maps.app.goo.gl/odK5tfgdqG9snir17?g_st=ic)

## Topo & Photos

![[Little Falls North Side - Smith Rock - Topo.png]]

Locate the crag by walking 100 past the west end of the Dihedrals: the rock is 35 high. black, with a distinctively tiered ceiling in the center.

### 163.  Zig Zag 5.8 Sport

At the left edge of Smith Rock there is a bolt line that goes up, then left, then up again to an anchor.  Some delicate moves on the traverse

### 164.  Unnamed  5.9 TR

A short inside corner leads to a slot in a chimney.  Above, separate finger cracks ascend opposite sides of two large chockstones.

### 165 .  Unnamed  5.11c TR

From an obvious block a few feet right of the corner a series of small widely-spaced edges lead to good holds in a horizontal crack. A thin vertical seam reaches the next horizontal.  Somewhat easier moves go straight up to the finish.

### 166.  Shock the Monkey  5.11d G***

The most acrobatic sequence of moves in Little Falls.  Climb the corner and by-pass the huge roof on the left. An instant classic put up in semi-traditional style by Ming Lin.

### 167.  Power Outage  5.10d G**

Genetics are more important than style in reaching over the double roof of this fine route. Climb a series of small corners to a thin crack splitting the roof. A bolt above (placed on the lead) protects the crux.

### 168.  Prepare to Meet Thy God  5.7 PG

This is the obvious finger/hand crack. Harder than it looks but often soloed.

### 169.  Unnamed  5.7 TR

Boulder a few moves near the center of the blackish face just right of Power Outage.  Once you have established your feet at the horizontal crack climb past a triangle and more or less directly over the ceiling above to finish.
