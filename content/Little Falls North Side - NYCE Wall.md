## Topo & Photos

![[Little Falls North Side - NYCE Wall - Photo.png]]

From the eastern edge of the dihedrals, walk east along the tracks a little less than 100 yards.  Turn north and head into the woods.  Look for the small wall shown above about 100 ft in.  The NYCE wall is on private property, but not on the large private parcel we have been discussing throughout the guide.

### 216.  Local's Trick  5.11 TR

The face left of the buttress on the west side of NYCE rock has been top-roped in modem style. Finish via the obvious crack.

### 217.  NYCE Face  5.12b/c G***

Two bolts in the center of NYCE rock mark this excellent and very demanding line. Though just 35' in length, there is much to ponder on this route.  Long reaches and a clever move or two, then after the second bolt go left at the horizontal crack and then up.
