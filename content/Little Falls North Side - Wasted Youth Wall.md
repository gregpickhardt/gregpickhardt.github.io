## Topo & Photos

![[Little Falls North Side - Wasted Youth Wall - Topo Detail.png]]

The Wasted Youth Wall is the first crag east of the old gravel factory.  It is apparently a pretty blank face that is largely unexplored.  I know little about it at this time.

### 131.  Lost Children  5.8 R

This route boulders left off the terrace (near the giant oak tree) of Wasted Youth Wall, avoiding the main face and reaching a small platform 12' up. Ascend dirty corners to good cracks and the top. The protection at the bottom is iffy, but then who would want to do a second ascent anyway?

### 132.  Unnamed (No information)

Scramble up the grass and dirt ramp left of Doktor Ratt.  Directly behind a maple tree a left-facing corner forms an arch topped by a roof. Climb the corner (same loose rock). then traverse left to a thin vertical crack. Power through the roof and reach right for a block.  Easier climbing in the smaller left-facing corner system above brings you to the top.
